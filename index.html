About

I was born in Pamplona/Iruña, Navarra, and I now work in Madrid. I am a researcher at Nokia Transformation, Engineering & Consulting Services Spain (TECSS), where I work in the XR Lab on the Incluverso 5G project. I am also a PhD student at the Grupo de Tratamiento de Imágenes (GTI) of the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM).

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Telecommunication Engineering from the Universidad Pública de Navarra (UPNA) and a Master’s degree in Telecommunication Engineering from UPM. During an Erasmus stay at ESIEE Paris, I developed a brain-computer interface for prosthesis control. My master’s thesis was an immersive therapy tool for people with stair phobia and intellectual disabilities.
