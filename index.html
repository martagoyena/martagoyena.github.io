About

I’m a researcher and PhD student at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), where I evaluate different interaction methods in XR: head-based input, controllers, hands and haptic gloves. I’m passionate about interaction design and about making XR work for everyone.

My PhD is with the Grupo de Tratamiento de Imágenes (GTI) at UPM. Previously, in the XR Lab at Nokia TECSS, I worked on the Incluverso 5G project, building XR tools for behavioral therapy and cognitive training, including job reintegration for people with intellectual disabilities. My methods combine inclusive design, inclusive questionnaires and qualitative analysis.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree (UPNA) and a Master’s degree (UPM) in Telecommunication Engineering. Earlier work includes a brain-computer interface for prosthesis control, developed during an Erasmus stay at ESIEE Paris, and my master’s thesis, an immersive therapy tool for people with stair phobia and intellectual disabilities.
