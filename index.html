About

I’m a researcher and PhD student at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), where I investigate interaction methods in XR, including head-based input, controllers, hand tracking and haptic gloves. I’m interested in how interaction design can make immersive experiences more usable and accessible.

Previously, in the XR Lab at Nokia TECSS, I worked on the Incluverso 5G project, which explored XR tools for behavioral therapy and cognitive training, including employment-related training for people with intellectual disabilities. My work combines inclusive design, user evaluation, questionnaires and qualitative analysis.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree from the Public University of Navarre (UPNA) and a Master’s degree in Telecommunication Engineering from UPM. Earlier work includes a brain-computer interface for prosthesis control, developed during an Erasmus stay at ESIEE Paris.
